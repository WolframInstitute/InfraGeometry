## Abstract

*Abstract written by Pavel Hajek on 30 October 2026.*

[Infra Substrates and Observers](paclet:WolframInstitute/InfraGeometry/guide/InfraSubstrates) gives the basic point of view: a graph is a **discrete substrate** seen by an observer.

[Euclidean Infrageometry](paclet:WolframInstitute/InfraGeometry/guide/EuclideanInfrageometry) is the analogue of the **synthetic approach** to classical geometry. It studies objects and constructions, and not so much the substrate.

[Riemannian Infrageometry](paclet:WolframInstitute/InfraGeometry/guide/RiemannianInfrageometry) studies the **substrate itself** from a geometric point of view.

[Infra Topology](paclet:WolframInstitute/InfraGeometry/guide/InfraTopology) studies the substrate from the **global perspective**.

[Infra Fiber Bundles](paclet:WolframInstitute/InfraGeometry/guide/InfraFiberBundles) studies **relations between different graphs**.

[Infra Analysis](paclet:WolframInstitute/InfraGeometry/guide/InfraAnalysis) is mainly **integration and differentiation**, and related operations.
