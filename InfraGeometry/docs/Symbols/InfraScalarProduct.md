## Usage

`InfraScalarProduct[graph, o, u, v]` returns the base-point-relative scalar product d(o,u) d(o,v) cos(theta_k).

## Details & Options

Options:

| Option | Values |
|---|---|
| `Method` | "Alexandrov" (default; the k = 0 polar form), {"Alexandrov", "Curvature" -> k}, "Parallelogram" |
