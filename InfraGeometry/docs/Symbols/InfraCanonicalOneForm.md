## Usage

`InfraCanonicalOneForm[g, {x, ..., v}, {y, ...}]` gives the canonical 1-form at the vector x -> v on the step to y: the pairing (d(x,v)^2 + d(x,y)^2 - d(v,y)^2)/2.
