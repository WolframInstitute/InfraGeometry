## Usage

`FindInfraShellCenter[graph, shell]` returns {center, radii}: a weighted InfraPoint of centers and a sorted radius list.

## Details & Options

Options:

| Option | Values |
|---|---|
| `Method` | "MaximalChordsBisectors" (default; sub-options "Maximality", "Distance", "Parity") \| "EquidistantPoints" |
