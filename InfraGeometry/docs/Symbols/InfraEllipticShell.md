## Usage

`InfraEllipticShell[{set}]` is the unary form (one elliptic-shell vertex set); InfraEllipticShell[{set1, ..., setk}] is the multi-realisation form.

## Details & Options

Find* returns one wrapper carrying the requested realisations.
