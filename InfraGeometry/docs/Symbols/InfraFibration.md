## Usage

`InfraFibration[total, proj]` is a fibration of graphs: the total graph with its projection to the base, an association or a function on the vertices of total.

`InfraFibration[fib]` gives the construction fib, such as InfraTangentBundle[g, r], as a literal InfraFibration, computing its total graph once.
