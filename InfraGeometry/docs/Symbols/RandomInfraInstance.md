## Usage

`RandomInfraInstance[scene, graph]` draws an admissible instance of a symbolic scene on a graph.

## Details & Options

The result is an InfraSceneInstance containing the bindings of one admissible construction branch.

This is the planned replacement for FindInfraScene. The scene sampler is not implemented yet.
