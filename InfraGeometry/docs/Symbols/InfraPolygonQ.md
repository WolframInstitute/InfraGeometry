## Usage

`InfraPolygonQ[graph, poly]` tests whether poly is a closed chain of geodesic InfraSegment sides (every leg a geodesic, consecutive legs sharing endpoints cyclically).

## Details & Options

Accepts an InfraPolygon wrapper or a bare leg list {seg1, ..., segn}.
