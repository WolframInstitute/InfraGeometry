## Usage

<code>[BallTopology]()[*g*, *r*]</code> gives the topology on the vertices of *g* generated by the closed balls of radius *r*, as a digraph: an edge *q* → *p* when the ball at *p* lies inside the ball at *q*, transitive edges removed.

## Details & Options

Definition: the closed ball is *B_r(v) = {u : d(u, v) ≤ r}*. The closed balls of radius *r* are a subbasis for the closed sets of a topology on the vertices, the *ball topology*. The closure of a vertex *p* is the intersection of the balls that contain it, *cl(p) = {q : B_r(p) ⊆ B_r(q)}*.

A finite topology is its *specialization preorder*, *q ≤ p* when *q ∈ cl(p)*: the closed sets are the sets closed under going down, the open sets the sets closed under going up. The digraph has an edge *q* → *p* for each such pair with *q ≠ p*, transitive edges removed. Vertices with equal balls lie on one directed cycle.

Every vertex of *g* is a vertex of the digraph, so the digraph alone carries the topology. [TopologicalClosure](), [TopologicalInterior](), [TopologicalBoundary](), [TopologicalNeighborhood]() and [ContinuousMapQ]() read it, and [TopologyGraph]() draws it.

At radius 0 the topology is discrete: no edges. At a radius of at least the diameter of a connected graph it is indiscrete: one directed cycle through every vertex. In between it only coarsens. The ball *B_(r+1)(v)* is the set of vertices within distance 1 of *B_r(v)*, so *B_r(p) ⊆ B_r(q)* gives *B_(r+1)(p) ⊆ B_(r+1)(q)*.

In a tiling no ball lies inside another ball of the same radius. On a patch of a tiling, therefore, an edge *q* → *p* needs the ball at *p* cut by the rim, and the topology is discrete away from the rim. On a tree the balls nest along every branch.

Option `"Dual"` -> `True` reverses the digraph. That is the preorder of the dual topology, whose open sets are the closed sets of this one.

## Basic Examples

The ball topology at radius 2 on the discretized plane, the square tiling and the hexagonal tiling. An arrow *q* → *p* says that the ball at *q* contains the ball at *p*. The balls nest only near the rim; inside, every vertex is open and closed.

```wl
GraphicsRow @ Table[
  With[
    {g = InfraSubstrate[name, "Small", "KeepCoordinates" -> True]},
    TopologyGraph[g, BallTopology[g, 2]]],
  {name, {"SquareMeshGraph", "SquareTilingGraph", "HexagonalTilingGraph"}}]
```

On a binary tree the balls nest along every branch, at radii 1, 2 and 3. From radius 2 on, two leaves with one parent have equal balls and form a directed 2-cycle.

```wl
With[
  {g = KaryTree[31]},
  GraphicsRow @ Table[TopologyGraph[g, BallTopology[g, r]], {r, 1, 3}]]
```

On a path the balls shrink towards each end. At radius 2 the two ends carry a chain each, and the middle vertex is isolated.

```wl
With[
  {g = PathGraph[Range[7]]},
  {topo = BallTopology[g, 2]},
  {TopologyGraph[g, topo], EdgeList[topo]}]
```

## Scope

At radius 0 the topology is discrete. At the diameter it is indiscrete, one directed cycle through all vertices.

```wl
With[
  {g = KaryTree[15]},
  GraphicsRow @ {TopologyGraph[g, BallTopology[g, 0]], TopologyGraph[g, BallTopology[g, GraphDiameter[g]]]}]
```

## Options

`"Dual"` -> `True` reverses every arrow: in the dual topology the closure of a vertex is the set of vertices whose ball lies inside its ball.

```wl
With[
  {g = KaryTree[31]},
  GraphicsRow @ {TopologyGraph[g, BallTopology[g, 2]], TopologyGraph[g, BallTopology[g, 2, "Dual" -> True]]}]
```

## Properties and Relations

The topology coarsens as the radius grows. The fraction of ordered pairs of distinct vertices that are comparable rises to 1 at the diameter.

```wl
ListLinePlot[
  Table[
    With[
      {g = InfraSubstrate[name, "Small"]},
      Table[{r, N[EdgeCount[TransitiveClosureGraph @ BallTopology[g, r]] / (VertexCount[g] (VertexCount[g] - 1))]}, {r, 0, GraphDiameter[g]}]],
    {name, {"SquareMeshGraph", "SquareTilingGraph", "HexagonalTilingGraph"}}],
  PlotLegends -> {"SquareMeshGraph", "SquareTilingGraph", "HexagonalTilingGraph"},
  AxesLabel -> {"r", "comparable"}]
```

The closure of a vertex is the intersection of the balls of radius *r* that contain it, the balls about the vertices of its own ball.

```wl
SeedRandom[1];
With[
  {g = InfraSubstrate["SquareTilingGraph", "Small", "KeepCoordinates" -> True]},
  {corner = First @ Select[VertexList[g], VertexDegree[g, #] == 1 &]},
  {closure = TopologicalClosure[BallTopology[g, 3], {corner}]},
  {InfraSubstrateHighlight[g, {Complement[closure, {corner}] -> StandardRed, corner -> StandardBlue}],
   closure == Intersection @@ (RandomInfraRepresentative[g, InfraBall[#, 3]] & /@ RandomInfraRepresentative[g, InfraBall[corner, 3]])}]
```
