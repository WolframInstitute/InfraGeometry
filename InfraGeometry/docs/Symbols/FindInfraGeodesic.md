## Usage

`FindInfraGeodesic[graph, p1, scale, kspec]` grows the geodesics at infra-scale scale from p1 -- FindInfraWalk at "InfraScale" -> scale with "Minimizing" among the rules; FindInfraGeodesic[graph, p1, p2, scale, kspec] keeps those ending at p2.
